End-to-End Resolution

Rapid Response Workflow

1
Incident Reported

Community reporters upload photos and location details of the emergency instantly.

2
Rule-based Priority

SOS priority uses assistance type and number of people. Community reports are not independently verified.

3
Responder Coordination

Volunteers can accept available SOS requests; NGOs allocate their own resources.

4
Field Resolution

Volunteers deploy, resolve the request, and submit photographic proof for admin verification.

Rule-based Estimates for Coordination

The disaster type comes from the reporter. Simple image-color or disaster-type rules provide an unvalidated severity estimate. These estimates can be wrong; they do not confirm damage or predict future disasters. SOS priority uses assistance type and number of people.
